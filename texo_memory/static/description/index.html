Texo - Memory

The working memory of an organisation, in its own Odoo Community: the project lives in Odoo itself (description, milestones, updates, tasks and their chatter); this module adds the rules an agent follows, the lessons learned without the customer and the map of the documentation. Agents read it through the Texo token and write it after the person's confirmation.

  • Rules on the organisation, a customer or a project, each with its state (draft, active, superseded, archived), confirmation and expiry dates; the rules of the organisation open every session.
  • Lessons that belong to nobody: findings written by the agents, curated lessons, procedures. No field points back to a customer; every hub member reads them; they survive a customer's opt-out.
  • Roles: a member writes drafts that a lead reviews; a lead's confirmed rule is active at once.
  • Leak filter: a finding that names a customer, a person, an amount or a record is refused and nothing is stored.
  • Context pack: the task with its chatter, chain task > project > customer with the rules and documents of each level, pending drafts, stale warnings and matching lessons, composed on the server.
  • Search: PostgreSQL full-text search on lessons, Italian and English stems side by side.
  • Routes behind the Texo token: rules, context, search, read, note, lesson, state.
  • Opt-out of a customer: their rules and documents go, the lessons stay, a certificate is posted on the request.
  • Weekly housekeeping: lessons that name a later customer are archived; near-duplicates become an activity for the leads.
